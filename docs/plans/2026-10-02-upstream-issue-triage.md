Date: 2026-10-02. Scope: all 160 open issues in wireviz/WireViz, checked against ClassicMiniDIY/WireViz v1.0.0. Bugs were reproduced against v1.0.0 where a repro took a few minutes (column *Verified*).

Status meanings:
- **STILL_APPLIES**: the problem or request exists in the fork.
- **FIXED**: resolved in the fork (0.5.0 ports or 1.0.0 fixes).
- **NOT_APPLICABLE**: `dev`-only, a support question already answered, user error, or a problem outside WireViz.
- **NEEDS_INFO**: the report has too little detail to act on.

Effort: S < 1 h, M about half a day, L days or needs a design. Value: H, M, L (users helped, severity, demand).
